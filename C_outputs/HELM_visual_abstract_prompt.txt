================================================

--------------------------------------------------------------------
SHORT PROMPT (paste this into the image AI)
--------------------------------------------------------------------
A clean, flat, professional scientific "graphical abstract" for a federated-learning
research paper, white background, minimal vector infographic style, thin rounded
rectangles, crisp sans-serif labels, muted navy-blue and grey with one magenta/pink
accent and a green highlight. Three zones connected left to right by arrows.
LEFT: four stacked rounded boxes labeled "Edge Cluster 1" (tagged "weak / mobile") to

"Policy federation (every T_RL, soft-update alpha)", plus a small grey box
"reward = accuracy - energy - Gini". Draw TWO distinct arrows from every edge cluster to
the cloud: a solid blue arrow labeled "model" and a dashed pink arrow labeled "policy".
RIGHT: four numbered callout boxes: "1 Hierarchical per-cluster RL selection (avoids
over-selection)", "2 Grounded energy (Joules) + fairness (Gini) in one reward",
"3 Policy federation is lossless: one shared policy, ~zero cost", "4 Pareto-optimal on
accuracy / energy / fairness". BOTTOM: a thin green banner reading "Real FEMNIST + MNIST:
-26 to -33% energy and -15 to -17% Gini vs centralized RL; -16 to -33% Gini vs Oort at
equal accuracy; advantage grows with scale; federation is free". Modern flat 2D
infographic, generous whitespace, no photorealism, no 3D, high resolution,
publication quality.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
STYLE NOTES
--------------------------------------------------------------------
- Flat 2D schematic, NOT a photo, NOT a 3D render.
- Palette: ink navy (#1a2233) text, blue (#2f6fb0) for cloud/model links, magenta/pink
  (#c72a6b) for "ours"/policy accents, green (#2e9e5b) for the results banner,
  light grey (#e9edf2) cluster boxes and grey (#6b7280) device dots.
- The KEY visual is the TWO different arrows (solid blue "model" vs dashed pink
  "policy") between clouds and clusters — make them clearly distinct.
- Large, legible labels; minimal text; lots of whitespace.
